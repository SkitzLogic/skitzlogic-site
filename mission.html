THE MISSION

Music can entertain.
Music can inspire.
Music can heal.
For me...
it's always been an escape.

More Than Music

Music has always been more than entertainment to me.

It's been a place to process life.
A way to quiet the noise.
A way to turn thoughts, emotions, victories, failures, hope, and heartbreak into something someone else might connect with.

That's why SkitzLogic exists.

Not to chase fame.
Not to chase money.
To create.
To learn.
To tell stories through sound.

Some songs are high-energy festival anthems.
Some are deeply personal.
Some are simply experiments born from curiosity.
Some are born from moments of joy.
Others from pain, loss, frustration, or hope.

No matter where they begin, every song is another chapter in a story that's still being written.

MAKE SOMEONE FEEL SOMETHING.

SECTION 2

Why I Create

I am fortunate.

I already have a career that provides for my family.
That gives me something many artists never get:
The freedom to create because I love creating.
Not because I have to.

Music sits at the intersection of creativity, technology, emotion, and curiosity.
Every new release is another opportunity to become a better producer, a better songwriter, and a better storyteller.

SECTION 3

A Promise

If SkitzLogic ever grows beyond my wildest expectations, I want that success to mean something.

After covering the costs required to continue creating music, every dollar of profit generated by SkitzLogic will be used to help other people.

  • Children growing up in difficult or unstable homes
  • Low-income families
  • Youth facing mental health challenges
  • Communities working to create brighter futures

Music has been an escape for me.
If it becomes hope for someone else, then SkitzLogic will have become far more than a music project.
